## Blackforest Breadpudding

Blackforest Breadpudding  
Ingredients        
Stale Bread
16 Oz
Eggs
8 Each
Milk
12 Oz
Chocolate chips
11 Oz
Cherry syrup
2 Oz
Vanilla Extract 
1 Tbsp
Heavy cream
12 Oz
Sugar
6 Oz
Cherries
8 Oz
Salt
1/2 Tbsp
Method
Preheat the oven to 325 F
Bring heavy cream to a simmer and pour over 9 oz of chocolate to melt.
In a bowl, beat the eggs, then add the milk, cream and chocolate mixture, Vanilla extract, sugar, and salt. Whisk well to combine.
Add stale bread pieces to the bowl. Stir to coat very well with the custard.
Add and stir in the Chocolate Chips to scatter on top.
Let everything sit and incorporate in the bowl for at least 20 minutes.
Portion 9 oz of the mixture into buttered 1 Lb tin molds, this should yield 8 portions total.
Sprinkle cherries on top 4-7 on each portion.  
Bake for 22 to 25 minutes  until the top looks set and the inside temp reaches 160f
Label with a 4 day shelf life and store in the fridge.

## Caramelized Onions

Caramelized Onions
Ingredients        
Spanish Onions (Small Dice)
8 Qt. (approximately 10-12 onions)
EVOO
2 oz
Butter
3 oz
Worcestershire 
1.5 oz 
Salt 
.1
Method
Dice onions evenly.
Add EVOO and Butter to a pot on Medium High.
Once the butter is completely melted, add in onions and reduce temp to medium low.  
Continuously stir onions to prevent them from burning. (add a splash of water to deglaze if needed)
Cook until they reach a deep caramelized brown color; it usually takes about 2 hours. 
When the onions are ¾ of the way cooked, add worcestershire and Salt. 
Reserve in a wide tray and let cool. Store in quart containers and label Caramelized Onion, 5 day shelf life.

## French Onion Dip

French Onion Dip
Ingredients
Yellow Onions
8 onions
Sour Cream
64 oz (4 lbs)
Mayo
32 oz (2 lbs)
Onion Powder
.51 oz
Dried Chives
1 Tbsp + 1 tsp
Salt
2 pinches
Pepper
2 pinches
Method
Carmelize Onions
-Large/ Medium dice onions
-Coat bottom of pan with thin layer of olive oil (approx 3 Tbsp)
-generously salt onions
-Set induction burner to 180 and cook onions until they are a deep golden brown color (2-3 hours)
-Stir often
-Transfer to a quarter sheet pan, top with parchment and refrigerate until completely cool
Assemble Dip
-Measure sour cream, mayo, onion powder, chives, salt & pepper directly into medium/large bowl on a scale
-Mix until incorporated
-Add onions & mix again
-Divide mixture into 6 oz portions in single use deli containers
-Using date gun, label with the use by date (5 days from today's date)
-Put French Onion stickers on tops and store in display fridge
-FIFO containers when storing
