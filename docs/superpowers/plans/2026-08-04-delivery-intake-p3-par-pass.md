**Goal:** The shelf-walk that IS the ordering system: staff record order-qty-to-par per SKU on their phone, the system derives draft orders per vendor AND soft on-hand anchors (`par_estimate` tier), completing the truth model (census > par_estimate > inferred) and lighting the shrinkage signal.

**Ground truth (live-verified 2026-08-04):** `vendor_items.weekday_par`/`weekend_par` EXIST and are SEEDED — 139 active SKUs with weekday pars, 20 with weekend (Cap Hill guide + BH guide, verbatim strings in notes). `vendor_ordering_details` EXISTS (method email|url|phone|portal|other + value + label; admin.vendors.ordering.* i18n live). `vendors.order_days`/`delivery_days` smallint[] (0094, JS getDay convention). NO par_pass tables. Highest migration 0171 → next 0172. `app/ordering/page.tsx` = placeholder to replace. Pack→oz conversion: `ozForRecipeInput`/`skuContentOz` (lib/recipe-math, public) — receiving.ts's `resolveReceivedOz` wrapper is private; the ordering lib builds its own thin wrapper on the public helpers. P2's inferred tier in `lib/counts.ts` (`loadInferredRows`, anchor-group batching) is the pattern for the new tier; `AnchorSource` union lives in lib/counts-shared.ts. AttentionItem kinds union at lib/midshift-shared.ts:66 + branches in AttentionBanner.tsx. Resend domain still unverified → draft orders deliver as copy/mailto/portal-link ONLY.

**Semantics (locked):** par and order qty are in the SKU's ORDER UNIT = its top pack level (chain root label when chained, else pack_format) — same implicit unit the seeded guide pars use. Par for a given day = weekend_par when the walk date's getDay() ∈ {5,6,0} (Fri/Sat/Sun) AND weekend_par is set, else weekday_par. implied_on_hand_order_units = max(par − order_qty, 0); implied oz = order-unit oz conversion × that (advisory-null when unconvertible — never fabricate). Shrinkage signal threshold: |implied_oz − computed_on_hand_oz| > max(25% of par_oz, one order-unit oz) — only when BOTH sides non-null.
