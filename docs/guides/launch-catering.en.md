## October 12: How do I follow catering during the shift?

### How do I check today's catering with the shop team?

Open **Catering** in **Mid-shift Pulse** for today's orders, their stages, tomorrow's outlook, and ezCater orders not rung into Toast. Open the section's detail for the link to the pipeline. Catering managers see order details, but sales amounts require a GM or higher.

### How do I leave a note for the next shift?

As a catering manager, open the **Handoff** detail to write a note and choose its audience. Use **Post note** to share it, or **Got it** to acknowledge a note you have read.

### How do I review an ezCater customization?

As a catering manager or a Manager of Operations or higher, open **ezCater item review** and expand an entry under **ezCater customization effects**. Choose what the option adds or removes from ingredient use, or which sub it selects in a package, then use **Approve customization**. Leave an uncertain option for review rather than guessing.

## October 8: How do I handle the new catering checks?

### How do I tell which shop owns an order?

Read the shop chip on each **Pipeline** card. To move an order, open its card, choose **Move to other shop**, pick the destination and a reason, then confirm. Demand and revenue follow the order. Prep already logged stays at the original shop. If ezCater later names a different shop, review the location before proceeding.

### How do I find ezCater orders missing from Toast?

Open **Not in Toast yet** and search by code, shop, or date. Ring the real order into Toast, then use the Toast cross-check to spot any remaining missing order or amount mismatch. ezCater is the order source; Toast is the cross-check.

### How do I review an ezCater item or Toast check?

In **Admin & setup → ezCater item review**, inspect the candidate and approve a match to a menu item, package, or direct CO-OPS item. Use **Pick another** if the suggestion is wrong. For a Toast-side review that is not an ezCater order, choose **Dismiss**, select a reason, and add a note when required. Do not map an uncertain item just to clear the queue.
