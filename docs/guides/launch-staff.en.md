## October 12: How do I keep up with the shift?

### How do I check what is happening in the shop?

Tap **Mid-Shift Pulse** in the dashboard menu for your work, station status, catering timing, food-safety reminders, and handoff notes for your team. The sections refresh while the page is open and visible. Tap a station on **Live shop floor** to see its details; you can switch between **3D view** and **Map view** when 3D is available. As crew, you see your own assignments without other people's details or sales amounts.

### How do I finish my shift on the board?

Use **End my shift** to release your station and unassign your open tasks. When the shop's closing checklist is finalized, the board also releases the stations and assignments held at closing. This does not clock you out of Toast.

## October 8: How do I run my shift?

### How do I see my station and tasks?

Open **My shift** on the dashboard. Tap **Tasks** or **Stations** to expand that part of the board. If you are a key holder, trainer, or higher, these headings read **Take or assign tasks** and **Take or assign stations**. Your station shows a named position. Fill the first position before taking another. The board says who has each task or position and who assigned, claimed, or took it. **Team today** is readable by everyone; you can see the whole team's coverage without changing their assignments.

### How do I take a position or get cover for a break?

Use **Claim / change station** for an open position. A key holder can assign someone else. Tap **Start break** when you leave your spot; it becomes open for cover. Tap **Back from break** when you return, then claim an open spot. Your old position is not reserved. If you clock out with work unfinished, the board marks it **Left open** so the team can cover it.

### How do I work the closing checklist?

Open **Closing checklist**. Sections collapse; the count on each tells you how many lines are done. The first unfinished section opens for you. Finish your lines in the station's section. When that section is done, the station closes on the team board. Tell your key holder about anything you could not finish.
