## October 12: How do I check the shop and follow up?

### How do I read the live shift overview?

Open **Mid-shift Pulse**; its sections refresh while the page is open and visible. As a key holder, trainer, or higher, you can see **People**, **Stations & tasks**, **Inventory**, catering details, and food-safety checks. The count in **People** includes Toast clock-ins that are not linked to an app account yet. A GM or higher also sees **Sales**; a Manager of Operations or higher can choose **Both shops**. Tap a station on **Live shop floor** for details, or switch between **3D view** and **Map view** when available.

### How do I check who is here and end someone's shift?

The team board shows whether someone is clocked in through Toast or active in CO-OPS. Without a Toast entry, a station or task can show a manager as present; signing in also counts for someone assigned to just one shop. As a key holder, trainer, or higher, use **End shift** for someone at your rank or below to release their station and open tasks. This does not clock them out of Toast.

### How do I connect Toast employees and find long-gone staff?

As a GM or higher, open **Toast employees** to link a Toast employee to the right person in CO-OPS or unlink a wrong match. Unlinked employees not seen for six months sit in a collapsed group. A Manager of Operations or higher also sees dormant and deactivated people grouped together in **User Management** when no filters are active.

### How do I use station closing times and staffing reminders?

In **Stations**, a GM or higher can set closing times and planned staffing reductions. As a key holder, trainer, or higher, you get a closing reminder when a station is due to close and a staffing reminder when it has more people than planned. Use **Choose who to release** or **Dismiss for today** on a staffing reminder. These times are reminders; they do not close stations or release people automatically.

### How do I review sales and download a report?

As a GM or higher, open **Sales**, or choose **Full Sales report** from the Pulse's sales detail. Choose **Summary**, **Items**, **Modifiers**, **Channels**, **Hour × weekday**, **Discounts**, or **Servers**; the hourly view shows a heatmap. You can also open **Checks** and **Catering**. Use **CSV** or **PDF** to download the report.

### How do I read an unfinished checklist in reports?

In **Reports Hub**, a checklist closed automatically shows **Auto-closed — not confirmed** instead of counting as done. Under- and over-par counts come from recorded opening and mid-day final checks.

### How do I tell purchased ingredients from prepared items?

AGMs, catering managers, prep managers, social media managers, and higher roles can open **Products** and the item catalog. A purchased product groups the same ingredient bought from different vendors. Follow **Used in:** on a **Purchased product**, or **Made from:** on a **Prep item**, to see the connected entries where links are shown.

### How do I check a selling price in menu costing?

AGMs, catering managers, prep managers, social media managers, and higher roles can open **Menu Costing**. Automatic selling prices come from Toast's in-store menu; a price entered by hand takes priority. A linked salad uses its sub's price when it has no price of its own.

### How do I find a customer?

As a GM or higher, open **Customers** for visits and spending in the shops you can see. Use the summary buttons, **Sort by**, and filters to narrow the list; **Include marketplace guests** includes guests from DoorDash and other delivery apps. A Manager of Operations or higher can choose the **Shop**.

### How do I read a customer's history or fix a wrong DoorDash link?

Open a customer's name for **Visits by month**, **Favourites**, and order history. You also see a shop breakdown when the visible history covers more than one shop, and a catering card when there are catering orders. Possible DoorDash links appear in a separate expandable group; those orders do not add to the totals above. An owner or higher can remove a wrong link with **Not the same person**.

### How do I download a marketing list?

As an owner or higher, use **Download opted-in list for this view** when an opted-in list is available. The download follows the current filters and includes only people who opted in to email marketing.

### How do I find catering customers who have gone quiet?

As a GM or higher, open **Catering accounts** from **Customers**. Use **Gone quiet only (over 90 days)** for accounts with at least two catering orders whose last catering order was more than 90 days ago.

## October 8: How do I run the new shift tools?

### How do I set stations and assign work?

Open **Assignments** from the dashboard. **Team today** is readable at every role level. Expand **Take or assign tasks** and **Take or assign stations**. Stations come from closing checklist sections and have On/Off status plus named positions; fill the first position before the next. Assign an eligible person to a task or position, then check the line showing who has it and who made the assignment. If you change an assignment made by someone more senior, choose a reason; **Other** needs a note. Use **Admin → Stations** to manage positions and station availability.

### What happens at break, clock-out, and closing?

**Start break** opens a position for cover; **Back from break** does not reserve the old spot. After clock-out, unfinished work shows **Left open** for reassignment. A station closes when its closing checklist section is complete. The checklist sections collapse and show completed counts; start with the first unfinished section.

### How do I save AM prep and record a batch?

Open **AM prep** and enter counts. A shared draft saves for this shop and day; watch for **Saved** or **Not saved — retrying** before leaving. Another authorized worker can pick up the same draft. For a batch recipe, count both **On the line** and **Bulk backup**. Record **Batches made**, **Came out to**, and **Bottled for the line**; if you exceed the minimum, choose a reason. Mark expired bulk backup as tossed. Bottle items keep their usual flow. A GM can review **Yield nudges** in Trends, update the recipe yield, or record retraining; an assigned key holder or higher marks their retraining task done in **My shift**.

### How do I receive a store run?

Open **Receiving**, choose the store-run path, and record the shop purchase and its receipt. You can enter a store run from anywhere, not only while standing at the shop. Check the receiving location before saving; the purchase belongs to that shop.

### How do I find and export reports?

Open **Reports Hub** and choose a family such as **Operations**, **Written reports**, **People**, **Inventory**, or **Menu costing**. Set the shop and date range, open the report, then use **CSV** for a spreadsheet or **PDF** for a printable copy where offered. Exports download files; scheduled email delivery is not active yet.
