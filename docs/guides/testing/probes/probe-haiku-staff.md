Clear shift dashboard with role-based information - Shows employees exactly what they need to know for their shift (station assignments, tasks, team status) without overwhelming them with irrelevant manager tools. This reduces confusion and helps new staff understand their responsibilities immediately.

2. Station and task assignment system - Employees can see which station they're assigned to and what tasks are pending, making it clear where they should be and what to work on. This prevents gaps in coverage and ensures accountability.
