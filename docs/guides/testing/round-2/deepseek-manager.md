## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. Opening Report ties last night's closer count to morning verification and prep-to-par. It makes the opener check every station and prep section against reality before doors open, so shortages are caught early.
2. Mid-Shift Pulse shows **Needs attention** first: uncovered stations, missed vendor cutoffs, and late reports in one live view. It tells the manager what to fix right now without hunting through pages.
3. Receiving lets you flag a line **Short**, **Over**, **Damaged**, or **Sub** and then opens a vendor credit automatically. It turns a truck mistake into money the shop can claim back.
4. Closing checklist links the daily reports (opening, AM prep, mid-day, cash, PM report) and ticks them themselves, plus a final walk-out and PIN confirmation. It makes sure the whole day is actually finished and signed before lock-up.
5. Reports Hub keeps a per-day, per-person record of opening, prep, cash, PM, and closing. It gives owners and managers a clear, dated trail of who did what and what happened each day.
