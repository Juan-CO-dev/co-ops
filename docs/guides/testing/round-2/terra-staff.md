## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant

1. Opening verification — checks stations and fridge temperatures before service, helping catch safety and readiness problems early.
2. Station closing checklist — gives each station specific accountable close-out work, so handoffs are consistent.
3. Mid-Shift Pulse — shows coverage, open tasks, food safety, and handoffs in one live view, helping the team react quickly.
4. Maintenance notes — records equipment concerns with a named owner and timestamp, so a warm fridge does not get forgotten.
5. Station claiming and shift status — shows who is covering each work area and lets crew take open positions, making staffing visible.
