## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. **Opening report with last night's closing check and fridge temps.** You walk each station, tick it, type every fridge temperature, and recount the prep items. The app saves each step as you go with your name and the time. A manager can see that the shop opened safely and what was really on the shelves, instead of trusting a clipboard.
2. **Opening prep and mid-day prep with par and "Prep need".** Every row shows Par, what is there, and how much to make, so a new crew member knows exactly how much to cook. When the number is off, the app asks for a reason, which tells managers about shortfalls early.
3. **Station claiming and the shift board.** A crew member can see which stations nobody has and claim one in a few taps, and the board shows who is covering what. This keeps the line covered and tells the team (and the Pulse) which stations are uncovered.
4. **Mid-Shift Pulse.** One page shows what needs action right now (here: mid-day prep late), which stations are covered or not, whether all 8 fridges are checked and in range, and catering due. A manager or crew member sees the state of the shop without walking around.
5. **Station closing checklist with notes and timed check-offs.** Each line is stamped with "you" and the time, lines can carry a note, and fridge temp lines take a number. The closing is spread over the day by station, and the AM prep count at night becomes tomorrow's "Closer count" for the opener, so the work links from night to morning.
