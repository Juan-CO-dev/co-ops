## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. Opening verification — records station checks, fridge temperatures, and opener counts in one workflow; it creates a strong food-safety and readiness record.
2. Mid-Shift Pulse — shows uncovered stations, low stock, fridge temperatures, and handoff notes; managers can see what needs attention quickly.
3. Receiving — records vendors, invoices, quantities, shortages, and vendor credits; it makes delivery discrepancies traceable.
4. Assignments board — shows who owns each task and lets managers take unassigned work; it clarifies responsibility during a shift.
5. Reports Hub — brings operations and receiving history together; managers can verify what happened and find past deliveries.
