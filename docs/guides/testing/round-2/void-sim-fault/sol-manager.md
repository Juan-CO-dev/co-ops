## Notes for the team

- What would make the guide better: Give practice values for counts, invoice, cash, and grading; explain what to do when a save or submission fails.
- What is hard in the app for no reason: Closing with incomplete work required 51 separate reason entries. Each reason box cut text off at about 40 characters.
- Steps that confused me even when the guide was right: **AM prep** is the night count; **Opening prep** is the morning report. The labels took a second read.
- Anything that looked broken: Opening entries did not persist; mid-day count would not submit; the delivery detail link returned 404. Reports Hub called the closing “Done” while also showing 51 skipped lines. The app allowed the shop to close at 6:14 AM in this practice run.

## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant

1. **Receiving:** Records a short delivery and opens a vendor credit, so the shop has a clear follow-up with the vendor.
2. **AM prep:** Saves and submits the closing count, giving tomorrow’s opener a starting inventory.
3. **Mid-Shift Pulse:** Shows uncovered stations and report status together, so a manager can spot gaps quickly.
4. **Cash Deposit:** Calculates the deposit around the $200 drawer float and requires a PIN signature, making the cash record accountable.
5. **Ordering walk:** Turns marked shelf needs into a vendor draft, giving the next order a reviewable starting point.
