## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. Opening and AM Prep reports — tracks what was counted, prepared, and left for the next crew; this makes handoffs measurable.
2. Station assignments and closing checklists — gives each person clear ownership and records completion.
3. Mid-Shift Pulse — shows coverage, open work, food safety, and handoff status in one place.
4. Temperature tracking and maintenance notes — records fridge temperatures and equipment concerns for managers.
5. Mid-day prep workflow — compares on-hand counts with par and records the prep needed to recover.
