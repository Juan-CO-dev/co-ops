## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. Opening verification — Checks stations, temperatures, and prep counts before service, reducing food-safety and readiness errors.
2. Prep tracking — Compares par, closer counts, and actual prep so managers can see shortages.
3. Mid-day prep — Turns a live count into a saved, finalized recovery plan after lunch.
4. AM Prep — Sends tomorrow’s on-hand counts directly to the next opening crew.
5. Closing checklist — Tracks station-specific closing work, temperatures, notes, and completion ownership.
