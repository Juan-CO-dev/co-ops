## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant

1. **Opening Report:** Records station checks, fridge readings, recounts, and prep against par; the next steps have a clear starting point.
2. **AM Prep:** Sends the closing count to the next morning’s crew, so they know what is left.
3. **Mid-day Prep:** Calculates what the line needs and records what was made, helping the crew return to par.
4. **Closing checklist:** Tracks each station’s finished jobs with names, times, and notes, making handoff easier.
5. **Mid-Shift Pulse:** Shows late work, station coverage, and fridge status in one place, helping the crew spot what needs attention.
