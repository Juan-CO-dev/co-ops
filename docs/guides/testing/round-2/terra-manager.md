## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant

1. Opening report — verifies every station, temperature, and prep level; it creates a disciplined, food-safe start.
2. Prep-to-par workflows — calculates needs and records what was made; it reduces line shortages and makes prep accountable.
3. Receiving with short flags and vendor credits — records a delivery and its shortage immediately; it protects the restaurant from lost credits.
4. Mid-Shift Pulse — shows uncovered stations, low stock, food safety, and unfinished reports together; it gives a manager a usable live picture.
5. Handoff notes — leaves visible, dated information for the next shift; it prevents important operational context from disappearing.
