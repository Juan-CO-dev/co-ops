## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant

1. **Opening report:** Connects station checks, fridge temperatures and prep needs so the opener can see what must be ready.
2. **Mid-day prep:** Counts against par and records what was made, helping the team rebuild the line after lunch.
3. **Receiving:** Records delivered quantities and a Short flag, giving managers a traceable vendor credit.
4. **Mid-Shift Pulse and handoff:** Shows uncovered stations and late cutoffs, then carries a note to the next shift.
5. **Closing checklist:** Brings counts, reports, cash and walk-out checks into one signed record for tomorrow’s opener.
