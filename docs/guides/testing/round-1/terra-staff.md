## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. Opening verification records every station and fridge temperature, helping crews catch unsafe or missed overnight work.
2. The Mid-Shift Pulse shows coverage, open tasks, food safety, catering, and handoff status in one place.
3. Station claiming makes ownership visible, so a crew member knows exactly where to work.
4. The closing checklist timestamps completed work and supports notes, creating clear handoff accountability.
5. Maintenance notes capture equipment problems with the reporter’s name and time, so managers can follow up.
