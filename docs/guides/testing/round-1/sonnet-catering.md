## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. **One board for every catering job (the pipeline).** Every lead sits in a stage column (Inquiry, Quote sent, Confirmed, Out for delivery, Completed, Lost), so everyone sees where each job stands. It matters because jobs don't get lost between phone calls and emails, and a manager can see at a glance what is coming.
2. **Quotes priced from your own menu and packages.** You pick a package or menu items, and the app adds up subtotal, tax, total and the deposit due (a $300 Light Lunch came to $330, with $82.50 up front). It matters because prices come from the shop's own menu, so the numbers are right every time.
3. **Confirming a lead feeds the kitchen's prep plan.** When I moved my lead to Confirmed, **Prep Demand** for P Street listed the job by date and flagged "Choose your sub - NEEDS PICK". It matters because the kitchen hears about big orders ahead of time and sees what is still undecided, instead of finding out that morning.
4. **A numbers page for the whole month.** **Catering Insights** shows new leads, booked events, money upcoming and completed, a calendar of booked days, and CSV and PDF downloads. It matters because the manager can read how catering is doing in one place and print it.
5. **A customer list built as you work.** Saving a quote with a client email put a new person in **Catering Customers**, grouped under their company, with their email. It matters because you build a catering client list as a by-product of doing the work, and you don't have to type it twice.
