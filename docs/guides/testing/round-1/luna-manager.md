## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. Opening verification and prep tracking — connects last night’s counts, fridge temperatures, recounts, and morning prep so the opener knows what is actually ready.
2. Mid-Shift Pulse — shows uncovered stations, food-safety temperatures, prep status, cutoffs, and handoff notes in one place.
3. Delivery receiving — records vendors, invoice details, quantities, shortages, and receipt-photo status.
4. Closing workflow — combines AM prep, cash, walk-out checks, incomplete-item reasons, and manager confirmation into one audit trail.
5. Ordering walk — turns shelf checks into vendor-specific draft orders with quantities, cutoffs, and purchase-order numbers.
