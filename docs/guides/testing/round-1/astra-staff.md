## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant

1. **Prep calculations:** Shows how much to make from counts and pars, helping crew replenish the line without doing the arithmetic.
2. **Closing accountability:** Records completed station jobs, names, times, temperatures, and notes so managers can see what remains.
3. **Overnight handoff counts:** Saves closing quantities and supports morning recounts, giving successive crews a shared stock record.
4. **Mid-Shift Pulse:** Brings uncovered stations, late tasks, and fridge status together so the team can spot priorities quickly.
5. **Maintenance records:** Keeps equipment concerns with the reporter’s name, giving managers a written issue to follow up.
