## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. **Catering Pipeline board** — it lays every lead out by stage from Inquiry to Completed, so the team can see all catering jobs and what needs follow-up at a glance.
2. **Catering Quotes builder** — it builds a priced quote line by line from packages, auto-calculates tax/total/deposit, and ties the quote to the lead so the kitchen knows what food is confirmed.
3. **Catering Insights** — it shows this week/month/30-day/all-time numbers plus a booked calendar, win rate, lead sources, and stage breakdown, so managers can track catering performance quickly.
4. **Catering Customers directory** — it groups customers by company with email and order/pipeline history, so the shop knows who orders and can look up their history in one tap.
5. **Catering Admin & setup** — it puts packages, menu, capacity, delivery zones, FAQ, prep demand, and LTO in one place, so a manager can configure the whole catering operation without hunting through menus.
