## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. Opening verification — Records station checks, fridge temperatures, and prep counts so the shop starts from a known condition.
2. Mid-Shift Pulse — Shows live station coverage, tasks, food safety, catering, and handoff status in one place.
3. Maintenance log — Gives managers a visible record of warm fridges and equipment problems.
4. Station assignment board — Lets crew claim open stations and shows coverage gaps.
5. Closing checklist — Tracks station jobs, completion times, counts, and notes by employee.
