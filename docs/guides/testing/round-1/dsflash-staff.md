## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. **Opening verification (Phase 1 of the Opening Report)** — one walk that checks every station, every fridge temperature and every prep count against last night's close, so the opener can catch a missed close or a warm fridge before the doors open. It matters because the morning crew otherwise starts the day blind.
2. **Opening prep entry (Phase 2), showing Par, Closer count and Prep need per row** — it tells the opener exactly how much to make and records who made it and when, so nothing is over- or under-prepped and any off-par amount has a written reason.
3. **The closing checklist, station by station, with notes and timestamps** — each crew member ticks their own jobs as they finish and can attach a note (I left one saying we were nearly out of pickles). It matters because the state of the shop is visible all day, not just at night, and the next shift can read what happened.
4. **AM Prep, the closing count sheet** — one place to record what is really left (on hand, back up, portioned or on the line) plus yes/no questions, which becomes the opener's "Closer count" the next morning. It matters because it is the shared number both shifts plan from.
5. **The Maintenance log** — it lists the last temperature for each fridge with OK / Out of range and lets anyone log a problem (I filed a note for the warm prep fridge). It matters because a warm fridge or broken equipment becomes a dated, named record a manager can act on instead of kitchen talk.
