## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant

1. **Connects closing reports and checks:** Filed reports tick their closing lines automatically, helping managers see what still needs doing before locking up.
2. **Calculates prep to par:** Opening and mid-day counts produce clear prep quantities, helping the crew replenish without guessing.
3. **Records delivery shortages:** The delivery retains quantities, invoice details, notes, and an open credit, giving managers something concrete to follow up.
4. **Shows shift problems and preserves handoffs:** Pulse exposes uncovered stations, overdue closing, and fridge readings; posted notes carry the context to the next shift.
5. **Reconciles and signs cash:** It subtracts the register float, compares the deposit with projected cash, and records the signer, reducing arithmetic and accountability gaps.
