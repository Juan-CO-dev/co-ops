## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant

1. **Lead pipeline:** Keeps the event, shop, headcount, notes, owner and stage together, so managers can see what needs follow-up.
2. **Package quote builder:** Prices the lunch and calculates tax, total and deposit, so managers can give customers a clear amount.
3. **Prep Demand:** Shows upcoming confirmed food needs and flags missing choices, so the kitchen can spot planning gaps.
4. **Catering Insights:** Shows leads, bookings, money and a calendar for the month, so managers can track demand.
5. **Catering Customers:** Groups contacts by company and opens their history, so staff can find returning customers.
