## Notes for the team
- What would make the guide better: Add a “minimum viable opening” time estimate and explain that an absent closing count creates 38 manual recount/prep entries.
- What is hard in the app for no reason: Repeated unlabeled “See more” controls make the Handoff destination hard to select.
- Steps that confused me even when the guide was right: The opening report kept the Phase 1 title while Phase 2 was active; the guide warns about this, but it still feels wrong.
- Anything that looked broken: The Pulse showed “Tasks: 0 done · 1 left” while the Handoff card showed five reports left; that status relationship was unclear.

## The 5 best things CO-OPS does for a restaurant
1. Opening verification — records station checks, temperatures, and recounts so food safety and readiness are visible.
2. Receiving with vendor credits — logs a short delivery and automatically surfaces the open vendor credit.
3. Mid-Shift Pulse — puts uncovered stations, prep status, order cutoffs, and food-safety readings in one view.
4. Assignment board — clearly shows unassigned daily operational work and who took each task.
5. Delivery history — keeps invoice number, vendor, manager, time, item count, and receipt-photo status together.
